# ADR 0014: Hash salt `alethea-v1`, embedder id `hashed-ngram-v2:512`, semantic floor 0.35

Status: Accepted (owner decisions; measurements in `docs/architecture/lessons.md`, "Salt rename") | Touches ADR 0001 (row 7), ADR 0010

**Context.** The hashed embedder's blake2b salt still carried the pre-rename project name. The owner decided to rename it to `alethea-v1`. Changing the salt re-rolls every hashed n-gram vector, and stored vectors are keyed by `embedder_id`; with an unchanged id, an old database would silently mix vectors of two different hash functions in one cosine comparison.

**Decision.**
1. `vectorize.SALT` (and the unused `config.HASH_SALT`, pinned equal by a test) is `b"alethea-v1"`.
2. The hashed embedder id is `hashed-ngram-v2:512`. The number versions the vector algorithm (salt, features, weights, dimension) and must be bumped with any change to it; `tests/test_hash_salt_migration.py` pins a digest of one vector to the id. The old id is listed in `vectorize.RETIRED_HASHED_IDS`: `embed_batch` never labels current vectors with it. Rows that carry the old id are simply "another embedder" to the machinery that already exists: recall's vector channel, dedup and recombine compare only equal ids, the full-text channel still reaches them, and `mnemo reembed` (hash target, local, no consent needed) rewrites them (identity memories only with `--include-identity`). No automatic migration, nothing is rewritten at startup.
3. `recall.SEM_MIN_COS` (the semantic relevance floor of the hashed embedder) moves from 0.30 to 0.35. Reason: under the new salt one of the 19 negative cues ("who won the football world cup in 2018") reached core cosine 0.314 with an unrelated memory and was returned. Over 200 random salts the 0.30 floor leaks at least one negative for 11.5% of salts and 0.35 for 3%; the previous salt was a lucky draw. Recall@3, top-1, precision@3 and the untuned held-out score do not change at any floor between 0.30 and 0.40 on the committed sets. `Embedder.SEM_MIN_COS` (default for plugin embedders, unmeasured) stays 0.30.

**Unchanged on purpose.** `NEAR_DUPLICATE` 0.88 and its clamp in `encode.threshold`, `ENCODE_THRESHOLD`, the recombine band, `SEM_GAIN`, the lexical coverage rule and every veto (identifier, negation/antonym, substitution, order, case). Checked: all 68 adversarial and restatement pairs satisfy the 0.88 contract under 202 salts (non-merge pairs max 0.550 because a veto damps them, merge pairs 1.000).

**Consequences.** A development database written before this change loses semantic recall for its old rows (full-text recall still works) until `mnemo reembed` runs. Raising the floor makes the semantic channel alone less sensitive: matches that share no word with the cue and have a cosine between 0.30 and 0.35 are no longer returned; the committed corpus has none (most related pairs are carried by the lexical coverage rule). The floor is still tuned against the committed negatives; a real embedding model remains the proper fix.
